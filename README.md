Vice City Cover Studio is an offline web app that turns any photo and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — hero-and-grid, comic-strip and split-duo layouts, a Vice City sunset or neon-night grade, bold outlined lettering, code-drawn stickers, and one-click PNG exports at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Everything runs locally in the browser; photos never leave the machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — load the sample photo (or drop in your own), pick a layout, grade and caption, and export your cover.
